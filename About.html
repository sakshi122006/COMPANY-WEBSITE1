Our Story

Founded in 2011, SAKSHI'S Company started with a simple vision: to help businesses transform through technology and innovation. What began as a small consulting team has grown into a trusted partner for over 1000 businesses across 5 continents.

We've worked with startups, established corporations, and everything in between. Each project taught us something new, and each client relationship strengthened our commitment to excellence.

Today, we're proud of our diverse team of 500+ professionals who share our passion for delivering exceptional results and creating meaningful impact.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide innovative, reliable and practical solutions that help businesses grow and adapt in a rapidly changing digital landscape. We're committed to understanding each client's unique challenges and delivering tailored strategies that drive real business value.

Our Vision

To become a trusted technology and business partner for organizations worldwide. We envision a future where businesses of all sizes have access to world-class consulting, development, and marketing expertise that empowers them to achieve their goals.
